Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers the HandoffCheck web app at handoffcheck.vercel.app and its Windows desktop client. The project maintainer can be reached at moiz44386@gmail.com. It explains what the service processes, where information goes and how to request access or deletion.

Synthetic-data pilot. This deployment uses unpaid Gemini quota for optional AI features. Use synthetic or demo request content only. Do not submit confidential company information, sensitive personal data or real customer records.

1. Information we process

  • Account and workspace information: Google account identifier, email, name and basic profile information supplied at sign-in; invitations, roles and membership status.
  • Request content: feature descriptions, criteria, supporting links, uploaded screenshots and PDFs, comments, revisions, reviewer assignments, decisions and implementation briefs.
  • AI interactions: drafting messages, proposed fields, clarification questions, review results, summaries, QA drafts and related usage records.
  • Integration records: connected Form, Sheet and Doc identifiers, import/export status, Linear destinations and confirmed ticket payloads, Gmail connection metadata and reminder-delivery status.
  • Operational information: authentication events, timestamps, audit events, errors and usage needed to operate and troubleshoot the service. Hosting and service providers may also process IP addresses and request metadata.

2. Why information is used and who can access it

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce invitations and permissions, store requests, support review and approval, generate handoffs, run selected integrations and reminders, and investigate errors or abuse.

Within the app, authors access their requests and current assigned leads access submitted requests according to the workflow. Drafts are author-only. Workspace administrators manage invitations and roles; that role does not automatically grant access to every request's content. Authorized service operators and infrastructure providers may access data where necessary to maintain the service, investigate a reported problem or meet a legal obligation.

HandoffCheck does not sell personal information, run advertising or add advertising trackers. Google API data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Connecting Google does not authorize sale of that data, advertising use or unrelated reuse.

3. Google sign-in, Workspace and Gmail

Google sign-in

Google supplies basic identity information to Supabase Authentication so HandoffCheck can establish a session and check workspace membership. HandoffCheck does not receive your Google password. Signing in does not itself grant Gmail or Google Drive access.

Google Workspace workflow

A workspace owner can separately authorize an Apps Script integration to create and update intake Forms, tracking Sheets and approved Google Docs. The integration reads form answers and respondent email to import requests, updates tracking information, and writes approved briefs to Google Docs. The form owner and people with access under Google's sharing settings may see those files and responses.

HandoffCheck's request permissions do not override Google Drive, Forms, Sheets or Docs sharing. Disabling the bridge does not delete existing Google files or revoke their separate sharing permissions.

Gmail reminders

Authors may separately grant gmail.send permission to send reminders from the same Google account they use to sign in. HandoffCheck does not request permission to read the author's inbox or scan existing messages. It uses Google identity information to check the connected sender.

Messages go only to current assigned reviewers with pending approval and a verified email address. Each email contains the feature title, revision and a link to the authenticated request. Google processes the sender, recipient and message contents. Sending permission and Google sign-in credentials are not included in AI prompts.

The server stores an encrypted Gmail refresh token and connection metadata. Short-lived access tokens are used to contact Google. Choose Disconnect Gmail for all my requests in a request's reminder schedule to remove the stored refresh token, invalidate pending connection attempts and cancel queued emails. Already-sent or in-flight messages cannot be recalled. You can also revoke access in Google Account connections.

4. Optional AI processing

When an author uses guided drafting or someone requests AI assistance, selected request fields or drafting conversation content are sent to Google Gemini. Workspace settings may also run review or assistance automatically after workflow events. Results are stored with the related request or draft interaction. AI proposals require human review; they do not approve requests.

This pilot uses unpaid Gemini quota. Under Google's applicable unpaid-service terms, Google may use submitted prompts and generated responses for product improvement and human review. HandoffCheck does not operate its own model-training system. Do not send confidential, sensitive or real customer information to these AI features.

The current adapter sends selected text fields and drafting messages. It does not fetch linked pages, extract uploaded PDFs or screenshots, or send Google passwords or Gmail authorization tokens to Gemini. Information you type into a request or chat can still become part of an AI prompt. The project maintainer must review provider configuration and data terms before expanding beyond synthetic-data use.

5. Hosting and other service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, database, private file storage and server functions.
  • Vercel: hosting and delivery of the web interface and public pages.
  • Google: sign-in, enabled Workspace integrations, Gmail reminders and Gemini processing described above.
  • Linear, when configured: receives the destination and approved brief content shown in the ticket preview when an authorized user confirms creation.

These providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices and may process information in countries outside your location. Copies exported to Google Docs, Linear, downloaded files or email are also subject to those destinations' access and retention settings.

6. Device storage and safeguards

The app uses browser or desktop storage to maintain authentication and personal interface choices, including hidden assistant messages. The local demo stores its synthetic records and files in browser storage. Clearing a chat view hides messages for that user; it does not delete the underlying saved review or approval records.

Hosted access uses HTTPS and server-side permission checks. Uploaded files use private storage and temporary download links. Gmail refresh tokens are encrypted on the server. Service operators and providers control infrastructure access; this is not end-to-end encrypted storage, and no system can guarantee that loss or unauthorized access will never occur.

7. Retention and deletion

Saved requests, revisions, approvals and audit records remain available for the workspace workflow until removed through an authorized maintenance or deletion process. Archiving is not deletion. Temporary drafting records, eligible AI artifacts and terminal email-delivery records have separate expiry or workspace retention rules; those rules do not automatically erase all request history.

To request a copy, correction or deletion of your account or associated data, email moiz44386@gmail.com from the address used for HandoffCheck. We may need to verify the request and coordinate with the workspace owner where records include other participants. There is currently no self-service account-deletion screen.

Some records may need to be retained for security, legal obligations or shared workspace records. Provider logs and backups follow their own retention processes, so deletion from the live app may not immediately remove all backup copies. Google Forms, Sheets, Docs, Linear issues, received emails and downloaded copies require separate handling by their owners or recipients. Disconnecting an integration does not delete those copies.

8. Your choices and contact

You can use the manual request form, ask the workspace administrator about AI settings, pause your reminder schedules, disconnect Gmail, revoke Google access, sign out and clear local browser data. Clearing device data does not delete hosted records. Stopping scheduled email does not erase messages already delivered.

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact the project maintainer at moiz44386@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, API keys or access tokens in your message.

9. Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Changes to Google permissions require the relevant authorization flow. Check this page before enabling a new integration or using the service with a new category of information.
