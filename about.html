How HandoffCheck works

  1. Describe the feature. Authors use the request form or an optional guided AI conversation to prepare a draft.
  2. Resolve missing details. Review checks and AI suggestions help identify unclear requirements. Authors decide which changes to accept.
  3. Get both approvals. Assigned product and engineering leads approve the same revision or request changes. AI cannot approve a feature.
  4. Prepare the handoff. Approved requests become implementation briefs, with Markdown download and optional Google Docs or confirmed Linear ticket creation.

Optional Google connections

Google sign-in identifies your account. An employee invitation is still required to enter the workspace. A workspace owner can connect Google Forms intake, Sheets tracking and Google Docs briefs through Apps Script.

Authors can separately connect Gmail to send repeating approval reminders from their own address to pending reviewers. This permission allows sending email; HandoffCheck does not request access to read the author's inbox.

Current pilot

This deployment is an invitation-only pilot for synthetic or demo request data. Its optional AI features use unpaid Google Gemini quota. Do not submit confidential company information, sensitive personal data or real customer records. Google's unpaid-service terms permit use of prompts and responses for product improvement and human review.
