1. Purpose and pilot scope

HandoffCheck helps product and engineering teams draft feature requests, resolve questions, record assigned lead approvals and prepare implementation briefs. Optional features include Google Workspace intake and exports, Gmail reminders, Gemini assistance and confirmed Linear ticket creation.

The current deployment is an invitation-only pilot for synthetic or demo request data. Do not submit confidential business information, sensitive personal data, real customer records, passwords, API keys or other secrets. Its unpaid Gemini setup is not approved by this pilot for processing those categories of information.

2. Accounts and workspace permissions

Use your own Google account and provide accurate account information. Google sign-in creates or identifies a sign-in account; workspace access also requires an active invitation. Keep your account secure, do not share sessions, and report suspected unauthorized access to the maintainer.

Workspace administrators control invitations and roles. Authors and assigned reviewers must act within their granted permissions. Do not bypass access controls, impersonate another person, collect other users' credentials or attempt to access requests outside your assignment. An invitation can be revoked by the workspace administrator.

Provider acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery. Timeouts can leave delivery unknown; the app does not automatically resend that uncertain attempt in the same cycle.

Disconnecting an integration does not remove previously exported documents, tickets, downloaded files or emails. Third-party terms and availability apply to those services.
